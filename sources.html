Every rule here was checked against the live platform and Redbelly's public pages on 22 October 2026. Nothing comes from Snapshot or older guides.

  • Governance platform: Overview, Wallet safety, New proposal and proposal pages with their Details tab.
  • The platform's public data at /api/proposals, /api/power and /api/identity: strategy, snapshot block, voting power and verified status.
  • Redbelly Access: its FAQ and account screens, including the 10 wallet limit.
  • Redbelly Developer Portal, User access: how users claim an access credential and enable their account.
  • Redbelly access contract on Routescan: the contract the platform checks, and the enable transactions used in the test above.

Every check, and how to repeat it, is in VERIFICATION.md.

This guide describes how voting works today. It does not propose changes to the voting rules.
