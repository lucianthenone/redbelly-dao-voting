Find the message the platform shows you.

Platform messages, what they mean and what to do
What you seeWhat it meansWhat to do
Connect your wallet to check eligibilityNo wallet is connected.Press Connect wallet.
No wallet detected or No wallet in this browserThis browser has no wallet.Install a browser wallet, open the site inside your wallet app, or use WalletConnect.
Switch to Redbelly NetworkYour wallet is on another network.Press it and approve the switch in your wallet.
Complete KYC to participate in DAO votingThis wallet is not verified and enabled.Verify and enable it at Redbelly Access, then press Check again. It can vote on proposals published after that.
Connect an address that was verified at the proposal snapshot.The wallet was not enabled at this proposal's snapshot.Use a wallet that was enabled before the proposal was published, or wait for the next proposal.
This address held no voting power at the proposal snapshot.The proposal is weighted by a balance, and the wallet held none at the snapshot block.Use a wallet that held the asset at that time.
Eligibility unavailableThe check did not finish.Press Check again. If it keeps failing, contact the proposal's organiser.
Voting closedThe deadline has passed.Votes can no longer be cast or changed.
