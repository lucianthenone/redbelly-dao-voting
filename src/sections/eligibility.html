Section 2

When eligibility is checked

Once per proposal, at its snapshot. The snapshot is taken the moment the proposal is published.

  • When a proposal is published, the platform records the latest Redbelly block. This is the snapshot block. Voting opens at the same time.
  • When you connect, the platform checks whether your wallet was enabled at that block. On proposals weighted by RBNT, it also reads your RBNT balance at that block.
  • The result is fixed until the proposal closes. Changes after the snapshot, such as enabling a wallet or buying RBNT, do not count for that proposal.
  • Each proposal's Details tab shows its snapshot block. The Proposal Resubmission Policy (#f832b) uses block 3208192, produced at 06:47 UTC on 30 September 2026. Voting opened at 06:49 UTC.
