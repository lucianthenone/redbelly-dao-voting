Section 3

Why a wallet verified after a proposal opens cannot vote on it

Because the platform checks your wallet as it was at the snapshot block, and at that block the wallet was not enabled yet.

Enabling a wallet is a transaction on Redbelly Network, recorded in a block. A wallet enabled after the snapshot block has no access record at that block, so the platform gives it 0 voting power on that proposal. The list of wallets that can vote is fixed when voting starts.

The wallet is not locked out. It can vote on every proposal published after it was enabled.

Tested on 22 October 2026

Four wallets enabled between 17 and 21 October 2026 show as verified on the platform today, yet each has 0 voting power on the Proposal Resubmission Policy, whose snapshot is from 30 September. The Redbelly access contract shows them as not enabled at block 3208192.
