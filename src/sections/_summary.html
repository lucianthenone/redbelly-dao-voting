The short answers

Who can vote

Any wallet that is verified and enabled at Redbelly Access. Every proposal published on the platform today is open to verified wallets only.

When it is checked

At the snapshot, which is the moment a proposal is published. Your wallet must be enabled before that moment.

How many votes

One per verified wallet on proposals that count one verified address, one vote. Each wallet counts on its own.

What it costs

Nothing. A vote is a signed message, not a transaction. You pay no gas and nothing leaves your wallet.
