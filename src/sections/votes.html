Section 4

How many votes you get

One vote per verified wallet, on proposals that count one verified address, one vote. The DAO's three most recent proposals, from 2 and 30 September 2026, were counted this way.

Each proposal sets its own voting power. It is shown under the title and in the Details tab, next to Strategy.

Strategy native-balance (RBNT balance)The RBNT the wallet held at the snapshot block
Strategy erc20-balance (token balance)The wallet's balance of the named token at the snapshot block

In every case the wallet must also have been enabled at the snapshot, because every proposal published today is open to verified wallets only.

One vote per verified account

Redbelly Access calls each wallet address an account. Each verified account gets one ballot per proposal. A second ballot from the same wallet replaces the first.

Several wallets under one verification

Redbelly Access lets one verified person link up to 10 wallets, with Add another account or Link another wallet. Each linked wallet is enabled separately.

The platform does not group wallets by person. Each wallet that was enabled before the snapshot is a separate verified account with one vote. A wallet enabled later, or never enabled, has no vote on that proposal. The platform states this itself: verified addresses are not necessarily unique people.

Some proposals ask members to vote from one wallet only. That request comes from the proposal's author, and the platform does not enforce it.
