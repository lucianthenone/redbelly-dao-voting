Section 6

Known limits of the current setup

What to expect from the platform as it works today.

  • One vote per wallet, not per person. Up to 10 wallets can share one verification, and each enabled wallet votes on its own.
  • Identity quorum counts wallets. When a proposal needs a minimum number of verified voters, the platform treats that number as a floor on wallets, not proof of that many separate people.
  • No late entry. The snapshot is taken when the proposal is published. A wallet enabled even a minute later cannot vote on it.
  • Proposals are fixed once open. A published proposal cannot be edited.
  • Ballots are public. Each proposal lists every wallet that voted, its choice, its voting power and any reason.
  • Ballots are kept by the platform, not on the blockchain. Each carries the voter's signature so anyone can check it. IPFS receipts can take a while to appear.
  • Phones need a wallet app. Browser wallets only work inside a wallet app's own browser. WalletConnect works anywhere.
  • Older proposals came from Snapshot. Proposals from before 28 August 2026 were imported from the DAO's old Snapshot space and are marked as imported.
