Wallet, address
An app such as MetaMask that holds your keys. Its address, starting 0x, is your public ID.
Redbelly Access
The official portal where you verify your identity and enable wallets.
Averer
The accredited issuer that checks your identity.
Enabled wallet
A verified wallet switched on at Redbelly Access. The platform says it holds a Receptor credential. Receptor is Redbelly's identity protocol.
Snapshot block
The latest Redbelly block when a proposal is published. Eligibility and balances are read there.
Voting power
How much a ballot counts. Set by the proposal's Strategy.
Signature
Approving a message in your wallet to prove the vote is yours. The format is called EIP-712.
Gas
A fee for sending a transaction. Voting sends none.
IPFS receipt
A public copy of your signed ballot on IPFS, a shared file network.
Quorum
The minimum turnout a proposal needs. Identity quorum counts distinct verified voters.
