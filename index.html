Redbelly DAO member guide

How DAO voting works now

DAO voting has moved off Snapshot. Members now vote on the Redbelly DAO governance platform, which was approved for continued use on 22 September 2026. This guide explains the rules as the platform applies them today: who can vote, when that is decided, and how many votes you get.

The short answers

Who can vote

Any wallet that is verified and enabled at Redbelly Access. Every proposal published on the platform today is open to verified wallets only.

When it is checked

At the snapshot, which is the moment a proposal is published. Your wallet must be enabled before that moment.

How many votes

One per verified wallet on proposals that count one verified address, one vote. Each wallet counts on its own.

What it costs

Nothing. A vote is a signed message, not a transaction. You pay no gas and nothing leaves your wallet.

Step zero

Before you vote: verify a wallet

You do this once, at Redbelly Access, the official Redbelly Network portal. Skip ahead if your wallet already shows Account status: Enabled there.

  1. Connect your wallet. The portal names MetaMask and Coinbase Wallet. The address you connect becomes your public address on Redbelly Network.
  2. Verify your identity. Averer, the accredited issuer, checks your passport with a biometric check. The issuer covers the access fees.
  3. Enable the account. Once you are approved, press Enable account and sign in your wallet. When the portal shows Account status: Enabled, the wallet can vote on proposals published from then on.

The voting platform calls an enabled wallet one that holds a Receptor credential. In this guide, verified and enabled mean the same thing.

Section 1

How to vote, step by step

Once your wallet is enabled, voting takes about a minute. The steps use the button names on the platform.

  1. Connect. Open redbelly-governance.vercel.app, press Connect wallet and choose Browser wallet or WalletConnect. On a phone, use WalletConnect or your wallet app's own browser.
  2. Switch network if asked. If you see Switch to Redbelly Network, press it. The platform only works on Redbelly Network, chain ID 151.
  3. Open a proposal. Under Proposals, pick an open one. The line under its title shows how votes are counted, for example One verified address, one vote.
  4. Check you are eligible. The Cast your vote panel should say Eligible to vote. Any other message is explained in Why can't I vote?
  5. Choose. Pick your option. Some proposals let you tick several options, split your power or rank them, and the panel shows how. You can add a reason. It is public.
  6. Review and sign. Press Review and sign, check your choice, then press Sign in wallet and approve in your wallet. This is a signed message, not a transaction: no gas, and nothing can move. The Wallet safety page shows exactly what you sign.
  7. Done. The panel shows Vote recorded. A View IPFS receipt link appears when the receipt is published. Your vote counts even before it appears.

Changing your vote

Until voting closes, press Change vote and sign again. Only the latest ballot from each wallet counts.
